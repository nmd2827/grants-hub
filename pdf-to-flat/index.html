Recognized scores

1. Drop a file

Each page is rendered at a resolution matched to its staff size, deskewed, and staff lines are removed before symbols are read.

2. Check what was read

Open Preview to see the engraved result and a detection overlay of each page. Fix the instrument, key or time signature if needed. Measures that don't add up are flagged.

3. Import into Flat.io

Download .musicxml or .mxl and import it in Flat.io (New score → Import). Instrument sounds, transpositions and tempo come along.

Known limits: tuplets, grace notes, ties, slurs, lyrics, dynamics, multiple voices on one staff, mid-system clef, key or time changes, and one-line percussion staves aren't read. Handwritten or very low-resolution scans won't recognize reliably.
