Recognized scores

1. Drop a file

Pages are rendered at a resolution matched to the staff size, deskewed and locally thresholded (for scans), and staff lines are removed before symbols are read.

2. Check what was read

Open Preview for the engraved result and a detection overlay of every page. Bars whose beats don't add up are outlined so you know exactly where to look.

3. Import into Flat.io

Download .musicxml or .mxl and import it in Flat.io (New score → Import). Sounds, transpositions, tempo and dynamics come along.

Not read: handwritten music, more than two voices per staff, cross-staff beams, lyrics, articulations and ornaments, tempo changes, and 5-line drum-kit notehead shapes. Any recognizer makes mistakes on unusual engraving or poor scans, so check the flagged bars before you share the result.
